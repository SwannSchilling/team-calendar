Artist or studio account?
An artist account is for a tattoo artist. You get your own calendar and can work independently, or connect to one or more studios. When you book an appointment or take a deposit you choose which workplace it belongs to, and the appointments you have at a studio can also be managed by that studio’s manager.
A studio account is for the shop itself, and holds manager seats. Managers create and manage appointments on behalf of the artists connected to the studio — they never carry their own appointments.
Otherwise the two are the same product at the same price. Working solo now and joining a studio later changes nothing about your account or your data.
