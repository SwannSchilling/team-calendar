Frequently asked questions
Find answers to common questions about Anolla's salon booking software so you can evaluate the platform and start managing appointments with confidence.
keyboard_arrow_down How is Anolla different from typical salon booking software?
Many salon booking systems focus only on scheduling appointments and keeping calendars organised. Anolla brings bookings, customer relationship management, automation, flexible pricing and online payments together in one AI-powered platform. It supports both hourly appointments and multi-day reservations, works online and through mobile apps, can be used across different countries and offers multilingual functionality. This flexible salon management software is suitable for independent beauty professionals, growing salons and larger businesses with multiple staff members or locations.
keyboard_arrow_down Does Anolla support hourly and multi-day salon bookings at the same time?
Yes. This flexibility is one of Anolla’s key advantages over conventional salon booking systems. Instead of making beauty businesses choose between hourly and daily bookings, Anolla supports both within the same platform. You can schedule time based services such as haircuts, manicures, eyelash extensions and massages, as well as full day bookings for training courses, beauty days or studio rental. The appointment scheduling software also handles services and treatment packages with variable durations. Appointments, staff schedules and treatment room availability are brought together in one central calendar. Anolla is well suited to hybrid businesses, including beauty studios, spas, hair salons with training facilities, rentable treatment rooms and consultation based aesthetic clinics.
keyboard_arrow_down How does the Anolla AI assistant reduce salon administrative workload and human errors?
Anolla's automated AI customer support is available 24/7 and can resolve a large share of chat enquiries without staff intervention. It reduces repetitive administrative work, helps prevent human error and gives your team more time to serve clients and grow the salon.
keyboard_arrow_down How does Anolla’s dynamic pricing module help salons offer optimized prices to their clients?
Anolla’s dynamic pricing module automates pricing for services provided by hairdressers, beauticians, nail technicians, massage therapists and aesthetic practitioners. Business owners can create flexible rules that adjust treatment and package prices according to the time of day, day of the week, season, staff availability or customer loyalty status. Price changes are applied automatically based on your business rules and displayed in real time during online booking. Offering attractive rates during quieter periods can help fill gaps in the appointment calendar, while higher prices at peak times allow businesses to make the most of strong demand. This helps improve both capacity utilisation and overall profitability.
keyboard_arrow_down Is Anolla suitable both for an individual beauty professional and for a multi-location salon chain?
Yes. Anolla’s salon management software scales from independent hairdressers and nail technicians to multi location beauty salon groups, spas and aesthetic clinics. A solo practitioner can start with a powerful free plan covering essential online bookings, customer management and calendar tools. Larger businesses benefit from centralised management that brings appointments, staff schedules, service catalogues and reporting for every location into one system. The platform supports unlimited locations, beauty professionals and administrators. Flexible role and location based access control ensures that each stylist, beautician or receptionist sees only the schedule and information relevant to their work, while management retains a complete overview of the business.
keyboard_arrow_down Which tools and systems does Anolla salon booking software integrate with?
Anolla is built on an API first architecture and supports the integrations that modern salons, spas and aesthetic clinics rely on. Direct Stripe integration lets businesses collect deposits and booking fees or sell gift cards as part of the online booking process. Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Meta Pixel can be used to measure booking page conversions and campaign performance. Raw data can also be exported to BI and machine learning tools for detailed analysis of sales, service popularity and practitioner utilisation. Anolla’s API supports connections with other business systems, including point of sale platforms, accounting software, loyalty programmes and marketing automation tools.
keyboard_arrow_down What is included in the free plan and what do the paid salon features add?
Anolla's free plan includes the essential tools a hairdresser, beautician, nail technician or small beauty studio needs to accept online appointments and manage daily salon workflows. Ideal for independent professionals and small beauty teams, the free version includes a salon calendar, basic client management, booking confirmations and email reminders. Paid features add unlimited automation, including SMS reminders, waitlist filling and automatic offers for last-minute openings. Salons can also access advanced marketing and payment integrations, plus loyalty tools for managing rewards programmes, points, gift cards and personalised beauty or treatment plans.
keyboard_arrow_down How does Anolla’s usage-based pricing help salons mitigate business risks?
Anolla’s usage based pricing model helps hair salons, beauty studios, massage practices and aesthetic clinics reduce financial risk. Businesses do not have to carry high fixed monthly software costs when appointment volumes fall or demand is seasonally lower. Instead, costs scale with actual booking volume and active platform usage. This approach is particularly useful for seasonal beauty businesses, such as summer spas, beachside salons and treatment rooms that operate for only part of the year. It also suits businesses whose customer numbers fluctuate with promotions and changing trends. Owners can keep costs under control and pay according to how much the software and booking service are actually used.
keyboard_arrow_down How quickly can I get my salon up and running with Anolla?
Getting started takes only a few minutes, with a context aware AI assistant available around the clock to guide setup. Most businesses can create an account, configure their services and launch online appointment scheduling without technical assistance.
keyboard_arrow_down What makes Anolla’s salon booking software a future-proof solution?
Anolla is future ready cloud salon software with an extensible, modular architecture designed for hair salons, beauty studios, spas and aesthetic clinics. The platform continues to evolve through cloud infrastructure and AI powered learning, helping businesses improve capacity utilisation, receive smarter recommendations and automate repetitive administrative work. Unlike rigid systems, Anolla is built around a powerful API that makes it easier to add integrations with marketing, payment and point of sale platforms. Its modular design can also support an IoT ecosystem, including access control for rentable treatment rooms, equipment hire for lasers and aesthetic devices, and environmental sensors for monitoring room usage. This architecture enables new technology to be integrated as the industry develops, helping beauty businesses modernise their operations and support long term growth.
