For service providers
Features
Activate paid features as needed. Some features may require other paid features. VAT will be added to the prices. The exact price and terms are shown in the detailed view of each feature.
Core features
Automate customer booking confirmations and create a foundation for using Anolla's paid add-ons. The core feature activates automatic email confirmations and enables reminders and other paid add-ons.
View details
Business features
Coordinate teamwork, manage access, and monitor your business's operational status from one environment. The feature includes user and access rights management, administrator notifications, real-time operational statistics, and mobile management app notifications.
View details
Client booking
Provide customers with seamless, mobile-first self-service on the web and mobile devices so they can find, book, and manage a suitable service and time themselves. The customer application provides a quick overview and simple actions, follows your booking rules, and works on the web, iOS, and Android.
View details
Customer invoices
Automate the creation of payment documents for customers after online payments. Following supported payments, the system generates an invoice or payment document as a PDF, which the customer can download from the booking details and the service provider can manage in the customer invoices view.
View details
Memberships
Create loyalty and membership programs to offer personalized prices and benefits to different customer groups. Memberships allow you to define customer statuses and membership levels and associate them with special prices, passes, and other membership-based benefits.
View details
Passes
Sell and manage time-based access rights to make repeat visits easier for customers. Create punch cards, period passes, and other passes, sell or assign them to customers, and track their validity, uses, associated services, and supported automatic renewals.
View details
Mailchimp
Automate customer-based email marketing with Mailchimp and use only contacts with appropriate marketing consent in your campaigns. The integration allows you to synchronize customer contacts and customer groups with Mailchimp to create newsletters, targeted campaigns, and automated marketing workflows.
View details
SMS notifications
Reduce missed appointments by delivering time-sensitive booking information directly to customers' phones, even when they are not using email or the app. The paid SMS feature allows you to send booking confirmations and automatic reminders.
View details
Stripe payments
Accept online card payments from customers and automate payment-related booking workflows. The Stripe integration allows you to collect advance payments for services, automatically confirm paid bookings, and use recurring card payments for supported passes.
View details
Special service pricing
Optimize revenue and demand with automatically adjusted prices. Apply prices that differ from the standard price based on the date, time, day of the week, schedule, service, duration, or customer membership.
View details
Transactions
Consolidate payments and cash register activity into one view to make cash flows easier to track and account for. The virtual point-of-sale system combines automatic and manually entered transactions and provides an overview of the cash balance, turnover, and payment methods.
View details
Products
Keep product inventory and stock movements under control from one environment. Manage the product catalog, groups, prices, and units, and track sales, purchases, and stock movements by service location.
View details
Large-Screen View
Display the schedule and bookings conveniently on a TV or external monitor. The large-screen view gives the calendar more screen space, hides management menus, and reduces the amount of customer data shown on a public display.
View details
IFTTT Switches API
Automate smart devices based on booking start and end times. The IFTTT integration allows you to trigger external automation rules based on booking events, for example to control lighting, heating, or other connected devices.
View details
Help Center
Quickly get guidance, provide feedback, and report issues from one place. The Help Center brings together a 24/7 AI chat agent and guides for general usage questions and allows you to submit development suggestions, account- or data-specific issues, and reproducible bugs.
View details
Business profile
Manage your company's basic information and customer-facing business profile from one place. Set the brand name, primary business activity, country, time zone, and currency, and manage physical, region-based, and online service locations.
View details
Billing
Manage your Anolla subscription, payments, and invoices from one view. Billing brings together your subscription, automatic payments, and billing details and allows you to top up SMS credit and download invoices.
View details
Schedules
Manage the availability of employees, rooms, equipment, and other bookable resources from one place. Define regular working hours and date-specific exceptions so customers are always shown up-to-date bookable times.
View details
Calendar
Manage all bookings and related actions from one central view. Create, view, edit, cancel, and search for individual and group bookings, and manage the calendar view, participants, statuses, notifications, and change history.
View details
Users
Give team members exactly the access and notifications they need. Add users and assign their permissions for features, bookings, schedules, and service locations, and choose which bookings they receive notifications about.
View details
Clients
Keep customer information organized in a single CRM view and use it for more personalized service and segmentation. The customer database is updated automatically from bookings and brings together contact details, notes, groups, and memberships.
View details
Client notifications
Automate customer communication and keep customers informed about their bookings. Automatically send booking confirmations, reminders, and other supported booking-related email notifications.
View details
Real-time statistics
Monitor key business metrics from a single operational overview and make confident decisions based on up-to-date data. The dashboard brings together statistics on bookings, turnover, transactions, services, and new customers for the selected period.
View details
Services
Manage your entire service offering and booking logic from one place. Define the service name, type, price, duration, delivery method, and customer visibility, and link the service to one or more bookable schedules.
View details
Translations
Offer customers a multilingual booking experience, reach customers in different languages more easily, and expand into international sales. The Anolla user interface is localized in supported languages, and you can add translations for the names of your services and service groups.
View details
Link Manager
Direct customers to the right booking in as few steps as possible. Create customized booking links with a preselected language, service location, service group, service, schedule group, or schedule, and use them on your website, social media, and in campaigns.
View details
Minimum advance booking time
Give sufficient notice for services that require more preparation, transportation, or setup time and prevent last-minute bookings. Set a service-specific minimum advance booking time that overrides the general minimum advance booking time for that service.
View details
Add-on Service Booking
Allow customers to add suitable add-on services to the main service within a single booking. The system automatically calculates the total price and duration and only offers available times when the entire selection of services can be provided.
View details
Wordpress plugin
Add Anolla booking directly to your WordPress website and let customers start their booking from your site. The integration allows you to display Anolla's customer booking view on an existing WordPress page.
View details
Wix integration
Add Anolla booking directly to your Wix website and let customers start their booking from your site. The integration allows you to display Anolla's customer booking view on an existing Wix page.
View details
