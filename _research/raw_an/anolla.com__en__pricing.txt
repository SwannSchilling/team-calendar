Questions and answers: Anolla platform
Here you will find answers to the main questions about the Anolla platform. We cover finding and offering services, payments, and security. These answers help you better understand the platform’s capabilities.
keyboard_arrow_down Why choose Anolla – the all-in-one services platform?
Anolla connects clients looking for services and service providers into a single ecosystem. The platform enables bookings. It allows you to request quotes. It lets you order urgent jobs in real time. AI automatically matches clients and providers. This reduces search time by up to 70%¹. Everything happens securely and transparently from a single account.
keyboard_arrow_down How much does it cost to use the platform?
For clients, using the platform is free. Requests, offers, and bookings include no hidden fees. For service providers, joining is free. A fee applies only after the job is confirmed. The Boost service and additional integrations are optional paid modules.
keyboard_arrow_down How does Anolla help you quickly find the right service provider?
Anolla AI analyses your need. It matches it instantly with available service providers. You can compare offers. You can confirm the right order in minutes. The platform supports bookings, price requests, and urgent jobs in 25+ languages. A 24/7 virtual assistant helps at every step.
keyboard_arrow_down How does payment and transaction confirmation work?
Payments are made securely directly to the service provider. You can also pay via integrated payment solutions. Boost services and add-on services are billed automatically. All payments and confirmations are stored in the system. This ensures transparency and protection for both parties.
keyboard_arrow_down How does the Boost service increase the visibility and results of my request?
Boost sends your request directly to service providers in the area. AI highlights it in search results. It notifies suitable providers immediately. This increases the number of offers. It speeds up finding the right provider. Boost helps achieve up to 3x faster results and better prices.
keyboard_arrow_down How do I receive notifications about new orders or requests?
As a service provider, you receive instant notifications via email or mobile app. The platform allows you to configure notifications by region, service, or time period. Real-time notifications help you respond faster. They help increase the volume of orders.
keyboard_arrow_down Does the platform have background checks and trust ratings for service providers?
Service providers verify their personal and company information. Anolla AI evaluates activity history, customer feedback, and response speed. Verifications and ratings are displayed transparently on the profile. This increases trust. It helps you choose high-quality partners.
keyboard_arrow_down Does Anolla work in my area or internationally as well?
Yes, Anolla is a global platform. We have active service providers in 40+ countries. The system automatically localizes language, currency, and service categories. If there are no providers in your area yet, your request creates market demand for adding them.
keyboard_arrow_down How does Anolla AI better match the client with the service provider?
AI analyzes the client’s needs, location, and preferences. It matches this data in real time with suitable providers. The system learns from every transaction. This improves the accuracy of recommendations. It reduces search time. It increases customer satisfaction by up to 35%¹.
keyboard_arrow_down How does Anolla help grow my business and increase visibility?
The platform automatically displays your services in the relevant category and area. AI ranks active providers higher in search results. Real-time Boost services and customer reviews increase visibility and trust. Anolla helps grow your customer base and revenue without additional marketing.
keyboard_arrow_down Why is the Anolla platform a secure and reliable place for exchanging services?
All transactions take place over an encrypted connection. The platform uses automatic fraud detection. It also uses 24/7 monitoring. Anolla complies with GDPR requirements. The system stores data in cloud-based backups. Transparent ratings and a secure payment flow build trust for both parties.
