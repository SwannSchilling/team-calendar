Frequently asked questions
Frequently asked questions about Anolla tattoo booking software – clear answers to the everyday challenges of tattoo studios, from online bookings and waiting lists to deposits, cancellation policies and automated client communication, so you can adopt the system with confidence.
keyboard_arrow_down How does Anolla’s tattoo studio booking software differ from a standard appointment scheduler?
Many booking tools only handle time selection and calendar organization. Anolla is an AI-powered tattoo studio management software that brings appointments, client relationships, automation, flexible pricing, and online payments together in one place. It supports hourly sessions and multi-day bookings, works on the web and in mobile apps, and accommodates international, multilingual teams. Its flexible setup makes Anolla suitable for both independent tattoo artists and larger studios.
keyboard_arrow_down Does Anolla support simultaneous hourly and multi-day tattoo sessions?
Yes, this is one of Anolla’s most important advantages for tattoo studios. Unlike many beauty service booking systems, Anolla does not force you to choose only between an hourly or a day-based booking model. The platform lets you manage shorter hourly tattoo sessions, consultations, drawing and design prep times, as well as multi-day large-scale projects simultaneously. The studio can set up services with different durations—for example, quick small-symbol flash sessions, more detailed sleeve work, full-back projects, or cover-up pieces. All artists’ calendars, studio booths, and sessions of varying lengths are consolidated into a single booking calendar under one studio account. Anolla is a perfect match for hybrid tattoo studios that handle both short walk-in pieces and long, multi-session planned projects.
keyboard_arrow_down How does the Anolla AI assistant help reduce the administrative workload and human booking errors in a tattoo studio?
Anolla's automated AI support assistant is available 24/7 and can independently handle many common chat enquiries. Automated client communication reduces repetitive administrative work, helps prevent human error, and gives your team more time to create art, serve clients, and grow the studio.
keyboard_arrow_down How does Anolla’s dynamic pricing module help tattoo studios smartly optimize their prices?
The Anolla dynamic pricing module enables tattoo studios to automate their pricing strategy based on style, session length and demand. The studio can define precise pricing rules for different artist levels, times of day, days of week, seasons and customer segments – for example regular clients, new clients, walk-ins or large projects. All price changes are applied automatically according to the defined business rules, without the studio having to price each session manually. The system helps offer lower prices or special deals on low-demand days and times to fill open slots, and allows higher hourly or project-based rates on popular days and peak hours. As a result, the tattoo studio’s occupancy increases, idle hours are reduced and the overall profitability of each tattoo station improves.
keyboard_arrow_down Is Anolla suitable for both individual tattoo artists and tattoo studios operating as a chain?
Yes, Anolla is built to grow with your tattoo business – from a single freelance tattoo artist to a multi-location studio chain. A solo tattooist can start with a powerful free plan that includes all the essential tools for online booking, managing a client portfolio, and collecting deposits. Larger studios and tattoo parlors operating across several cities or countries can use a centralized dashboard to manage all artists, booths, queues, and bookings from one place. The platform lets you add an unlimited number of studio locations, tattoo artist profiles, and administrators. Flexible role- and location-based access control (RBAC) makes it possible to assign different permissions to studio managers, front-desk staff, apprentices, and guest artists for viewing or editing calendars, price lists, reports, and client data.
keyboard_arrow_down Which tools and platforms does Anolla's tattoo studio booking software integrate with?
Anolla is built with an API-first architecture that allows a tattoo studio to connect its booking system with other essential tools. For payment processing there is a direct integration with Stripe, which enables secure collection of deposits and full payments at the moment of online booking. For analytics the software supports integrations with Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Meta Pixel so the studio can measure booking sources, campaign ROI and ad effectiveness. Where needed, raw data can be exported to BI/ML tools to analyse artist occupancy, style popularity and average booking value. In addition, Anolla includes a powerful API and integration options with other systems used by the studio such as accounting software, marketing automation, loyalty programmes or website CMS platforms.
keyboard_arrow_down What does Anolla’s free plan include and what do the paid studio features add?
Anolla's free plan gives tattoo studios the essential tools needed to start accepting appointments online, including a dedicated booking page, a core calendar, client record management, and basic notifications. It is ideal for independent tattoo artists, new studios, and small teams that want to spend less time arranging sessions through social media and create a clear, professional booking process. Paid plans add unlimited automation, including multi-stage reminders, automatic aftercare instructions, and no-show prevention rules. They also include advanced integrations for payments, marketing, and analytics, plus loyalty features for returning-client discounts, reward points, and priority access to selected artists.
keyboard_arrow_down How does Anolla’s usage-based pricing help reduce a tattoo studio’s business risk?
Anolla’s usage-based pricing is designed specifically to reduce the financial risk of tattoo studios under conditions of fluctuating demand and seasonality. A studio doesn’t need to pay high fixed monthly fees during periods when there are fewer bookings – for example in low season or when the studio is just starting out and its client base is still growing. Software costs scale in proportion to the actual booking volume and system usage. This model is particularly suitable for studios with seasonal workload, salons located in regional tourist hubs, studios hosting guest artists and new artists whose schedules are still being established.
keyboard_arrow_down How quickly can a tattoo studio start working with Anolla?
Anolla takes only a few minutes to set up, with a context-aware AI assistant available 24/7 to guide you through the process. Most users can configure their account independently, making it quick and straightforward to start managing tattoo appointments.
keyboard_arrow_down What makes Anolla’s tattoo studio booking platform a future-proof solution?
Anolla’s architecture is built as a modular and extensible platform that grows with the needs of your tattoo studio. Cloud-based infrastructure and AI-driven learning enable the platform to continuously improve—from smart time planning to in-depth analysis of customer behavior. Unlike static calendar solutions, Anolla is built around a powerful API that makes it easy to integrate new features, payment solutions, and marketing tools. The platform also supports an IoT ecosystem—for example, door control solutions (self check-in for regular clients), equipment rental management, and sensors that help the studio monitor workspace usage. Thanks to this architecture, Anolla can quickly integrate the latest technologies, keeping your tattoo studio’s tools always a step ahead of the competition.
