The relevant customer organization, not InkLinka, is generally responsible for:

InkLinka does not independently verify that customer organizations have obtained valid notices, permissions, parental permissions, releases, or other legal authorizations for the data they choose to process through the Services.

15. Google API Data and Gmail Integrations
If a user or customer organization chooses to connect a Google account to InkLinka, InkLinka may access Google user data strictly to provide the email features requested by that user inside the InkLinka interface.
For connected Gmail accounts, InkLinka may access the Gmail address associated with the mailbox, message metadata, message headers, thread identifiers, message bodies, and attachments, and may send outbound emails or replies from the connected mailbox when initiated by the authorized user through InkLinka.
InkLinka uses this data to authenticate the mailbox connection, sync inbound emails into the workspace inbox, display messages and attachments in the application, match messages to conversation threads, send replies and connection test messages, maintain sync state, and secure or troubleshoot the integration.
OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, and related credentials for connected Google accounts are stored in encrypted form. Synced email content and related metadata may be stored in InkLinka systems so the customer organization can access the email workflow inside the product. Attachments may also be stored in secure cloud storage used by InkLinka when required to support the feature.
InkLinka does not sell Google user data, does not use Google user data for advertising, and does not use data obtained from Google Workspace APIs to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Google user data is shared only as necessary to operate the requested feature, for security purposes, to comply with law, or with service providers acting on InkLinka’s behalf under appropriate obligations.
Users can disconnect or delete a connected Google account from InkLinka. When a Gmail connection is disconnected or deleted, InkLinka attempts to revoke the stored Google refresh token and removes stored OAuth credentials from the active connection record. Previously synced workspace records may remain until deleted by the customer organization, by InkLinka under applicable retention rules, or upon a valid deletion request where required by law or contract.
The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
