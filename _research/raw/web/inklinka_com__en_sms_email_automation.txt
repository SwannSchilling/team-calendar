SMS & email automation FAQ
Questions before you automate client communication.
Clear answers about messages, consent forms, templates and aftercare workflows in InkLinka.
What can InkLinka send automatically? InkLinka can send booking confirmations, reminders, cancellation or reschedule updates, secure consent form links and aftercare messages when the relevant appointment or session event occurs.
Can the studio choose the channel and wording? Yes. The studio decides which notifications are active, whether SMS or email is used, what each template says and which connected sender delivers it.
How do digital consent forms work? The studio sends a secure form link by SMS or email. The client reviews and signs it online, while the completed form and its status remain connected to the relevant session.
Are tattoo and piercing aftercare messages different? Yes. Tattoo and piercing guidance can use separate templates, so the studio can approve instructions that match each service and its own care standards.
