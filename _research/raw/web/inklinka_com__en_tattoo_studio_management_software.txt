Tattoo studio operations should not be spread across DMs, calendars and spreadsheets
Many tattoo studios run daily operations across disconnected tools: Instagram DMs for booking requests, WhatsApp for references, Google Calendar for appointments, spreadsheets for deposits, private notes for client details and separate chats for artist coordination.
That creates operational friction. Managers lose time searching for context, artists miss project details, deposits become unclear, client history gets fragmented and the studio becomes harder to control as the team grows.
