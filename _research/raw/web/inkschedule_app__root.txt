Built for Tattoo Studios
Online Booking
Clients pick their service, date, and artist online. They pay a deposit to confirm. You get notified instantly.
Deposit Collection
Set a deposit amount and collect it automatically at booking via Stripe. No deposit means no appointment.
Artist Portfolios
A dedicated portfolio page for every artist. Clients browse styles and book directly from the page.
Smart Calendar
Day and week views built for long sessions. Block time off, drag to reschedule, view all artists at once.
Automatic Reminders
Clients get reminders 24h and 1h before their appointment. Aftercare instructions go out automatically after.
Waitlist
When you are fully booked, clients join a waitlist. They get notified the moment a slot opens.
AI Assistant
Ask the AI to plan your week, find gaps, or summarize revenue. Plain English, instant answers.
Digital Consent Forms
Clients sign waivers on their phone before arriving. No paper, no borrowed pens, no lost forms.
Revenue Analytics
Track revenue by artist, by service, and by month. Spot your busiest days and most profitable work.
Embeddable Widget
Paste one script tag on any website or link-in-bio. A full booking flow appears instantly.
Google Calendar Sync
Your personal calendar and studio calendar stay in sync. Personal blocks automatically close client slots.
Team Management
Invite artists with their own logins. Each artist manages their calendar, portfolio, and bookings independently.
Client Self-Service
Clients look up their booking with their email, reschedule, or sign forms. No account needed.
Multi-Session Bookings
For large pieces spanning multiple visits. Link sessions together and track progress across appointments.
