Calendar context
A regular calendar, like Google Calendar or a general CRM calendar, can show when the session starts. It usually does not show the full tattoo context.
Solution : In InkLinka, the appointment can be connected to the related tattoo project. The team can open the session and see the client, project, references, placement, notes, artist and session history in one place. The artist can also open the booked session and project from their mobile workspace.

FAQ
Practical questions before testing InkLinka
Can InkLinka replace our current calendar?
InkLinka is designed to add tattoo-specific context around appointments: clients, projects, references, deposits, artists and session history. You can test whether it fits your current scheduling workflow during the trial.
Can artists see what they need to prepare?
Yes. When an appointment is connected to a project, the artist can see the related client, references, notes, placement, project stage and session details.
Does InkLinka help with references stored in chats?
InkLinka keeps reference images and project files with the tattoo project, so the team does not need to rely only on Instagram, WhatsApp, screenshots or folders.
Can a new manager understand what is going on?
InkLinka helps keep the main connections between clients, projects, appointments, deposits and artists in the studio system, which makes handoff clearer.
