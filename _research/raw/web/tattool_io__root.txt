Built for how tattoo studios work
Each workflow is built around the work your studio already does: booking clients, coordinating artists and locations, collecting payments, sending forms, recording inks, and following up after the session.
Keep each appointment in context
A booking can hold the client, artist, location, notes, references, payments, consent forms, ink registrations, and activity, so you do not have to recreate the same appointment in separate tools.
Use only the parts you need
Start with scheduling and client records, then add payments, Flows, consent forms, ink registration, tasks, or reporting when they become useful to your studio. You do not need to set up every workflow before you begin.
Give each person the right access
Owners and admins manage organization settings. Managers and reception coordinate studio operations, while members focus on their own work. Calendar access can also be disabled for individual members.
See what is happening across the studio
Recorded bookings, payments, availability, and time off feed studio reports for revenue, utilization, capacity, targets, and future workload.
