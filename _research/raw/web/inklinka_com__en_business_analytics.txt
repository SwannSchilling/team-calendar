Clear answers before you start
What exactly does the AI Business Analyst do? It analyzes connected CRM data and identifies leads, clients, projects and payment situations that may require attention. It then recommends a specific next action.
Will it message clients automatically? No. The AI prepares the context and recommendation. A team member reviews and sends every message personally.
Will the manager know what to do first? Yes. Opportunities are ranked by relevance, urgency and available CRM evidence.
Can it use project and visit history? Yes. It can use connected requests, conversations, appointments, projects, sessions, payments, deposits and client history.
What if the CRM data is incomplete? The AI can identify missing context as a data quality opportunity. Recommendations are based only on available CRM evidence.
