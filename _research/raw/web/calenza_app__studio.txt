FAQ
Studio booking & management software, questions answered.
What software do tattoo studios use for booking and pay? Tattoo studios use software like Calenza to book sessions per artist, track each artist's commission, and take payment at checkout in one place. Booking, commissions and the till live in one system, so the schedule, pay and the books stay in sync without re-entering anything.
Does Calenza work as tattoo studio management software? Yes. Calenza handles the management side of a studio — a per-artist calendar, client records with visit history, services and products with stock, staff commissions, and reports that reconcile — so the studio runs from one system instead of several.
How does Calenza track artist commissions in a studio? Set each artist's pay as a commission percentage, a salary, or a hybrid. As tickets close, earnings tally per artist automatically against a server-side ledger that artists cannot alter, and payday is one action with a permanent, voidable record.
Does Calenza include studio checkout and point of sale? Yes. Calenza includes studio point of sale: ring up sessions and retail on one ticket, edit any price, apply discounts, and split payment across cash, card or transfer — with the books and commissions recalculating themselves and a cash drawer that reconciles at day's end.
Can Calenza handle walk-ins and prevent double-booking? Yes. You can record a walk-in in a few taps or schedule a session on the calendar, where each artist's bookings are laid out by time so the front desk can see availability at a glance and keep the day's slots clear.
