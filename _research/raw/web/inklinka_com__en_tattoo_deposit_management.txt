Problem
Tattoo deposits should not be tracked in screenshots, DMs and spreadsheets
Many tattoo studios collect deposits through different tools, then track the status manually. One client pays through a link, another confirms in a DM, another needs a reminder, and the deposit note ends up in a spreadsheet or private chat.
When deposit context is separated from the booking and appointment, managers waste time checking payment status, artists arrive without knowing whether the session is confirmed, and the studio risks missed follow-ups, no-shows and unclear client history.

Know which bookings are ready before the session
Deposit status is not only a finance detail. For tattoo studios, it helps confirm whether a booking is serious, whether the appointment should stay on the calendar and whether the studio needs to follow up before the client arrives.
Paid
The deposit is received and the booking can be treated as confirmed.
Pending
The client has not completed the deposit yet and may need a reminder.
Missing
The appointment may need follow-up before the studio treats it as confirmed.
Refunded
The deposit was returned and the studio can keep that context in the client history.
Adjusted
The deposit or payment context changed because of rescheduling, cancellation or studio decision.
