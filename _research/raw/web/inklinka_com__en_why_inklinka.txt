Tattoo studios do not run on appointments alone
Most studio software starts with a calendar. But tattoo work starts earlier: a client sends an idea, references, placement, size, style, availability, medical notes, budget questions, and deposit confirmation. If that information stays in DMs, the studio loses context before the session even begins.
Booking requests live in DMs
Client details, ideas and availability are buried in conversations.
References get lost
Images, screenshots and inspiration are scattered across chats and devices.
Deposits are disconnected
Payment status is often checked manually or tracked outside the booking.
Sessions lose context
Appointments appear in the calendar, but the project story is somewhere else.
The InkLinka difference
InkLinka connects the whole tattoo workflow
Instead of treating bookings, clients, files, deposits and sessions as separate pieces, InkLinka keeps them inside one project-based structure.
Every request becomes a project
A new booking request is not just a message. It becomes a structured tattoo project with client details, references, sessions, status and deposit visibility.
References stay where they belong
Reference images, notes and project details stay attached to the client and the tattoo project, not buried in old chats.
Sessions are connected to the project
Calendar events are not isolated appointments. They are part of the tattoo project history.
Deposits are visible in context
Deposit status is connected to the booking/project, so the team can quickly understand what is confirmed, pending or missing.
Client history is more than a profile
A client profile is useful, but InkLinka connects the client with projects, sessions, files, communication and payment context.
Built for studio + artists
InkLinka supports the studio workspace, artists, clients, sessions and operational visibility - not just one person's calendar.
Communication belongs to the project
Messages and important context should not live outside the workflow. InkLinka is designed to keep communication close to the project.
AI-ready by design
Because project, client, session, payment and communication data are structured, InkLinka can support smarter AI assistance for admins, artists and studio owners.

Why not just use a calendar, spreadsheet or salon CRM?
Why not just a calendar? A calendar shows time. It does not hold the full tattoo project: client ideas, references, sessions, deposits, notes and communication history.
Why not spreadsheets? Spreadsheets can track data, but they do not create a connected client and project workflow for the team.
Why not Instagram DMs? DMs are useful for starting conversations, but they are not a reliable system for managing confirmed tattoo projects.
Why not generic CRM? Generic CRMs manage contacts and pipelines, but tattoo work is visual, session-based and artist-specific.
Why not salon software? Tattoo studios do not work like quick beauty appointments. Tattoo projects often involve references, consultation, deposits, multiple sessions and long-term client history.
