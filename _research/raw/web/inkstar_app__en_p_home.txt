Deposit-first booking
Deposit-Based No-Show Protection
Every booking requires a deposit upfront. Automated appointment reminders go out via SMS and email at 24h and 2h before the session. If the client still doesn't show, you keep the deposit. Your tattoo studio schedule stays full, your income stays protected.

Smart scheduling
AI-Powered Appointment Scheduling
Intelligent scheduling software that fills gaps in your calendar automatically. Clients book appointments online, reschedule on their own, and your waitlist fills cancelled slots without you lifting a finger. Built for multi-artist tattoo studios, solo PMU practitioners, and busy barbershops alike.

Digital intake forms
Intake Forms and Digital Waivers
Industry-specific intake form templates with photo consent, medical history questionnaires, allergy fields, digital waivers, and legally binding e-signatures. Clients sign on their own phone via QR code before sitting in the chair. Every record is timestamped and stored securely for GDPR compliance.

Integrated payments
Integrated Payment Processing
Accept card payments, PayPal, and instant bank transfers directly through the app. Offer Klarna installment plans for large tattoo pieces. Deposits are collected automatically at booking, remaining balances charged after sessions. Payment reminders sent via text so you never chase money.

Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about using InkStar as an appointment planner app for tattoo, PMU, piercing, barber, and studio management workflows.
Is InkStar an appointment planner app or studio management software?
Both. InkStar starts with appointment planning and scheduling, then connects bookings to deposits, consent forms, client profiles, payments, reminders, reports, and offline studio work.
Who is this appointment planner app built for?
InkStar is built for tattoo artists, PMU professionals, piercers, barbers, beauty studios, and small teams that need more than a generic calendar.
Can clients book through Instagram, my website, or a public booking page?
Yes. You can use booking links and a public booking page so clients can request appointments from your social profiles, website, or other online touchpoints.
Does InkStar support deposits and no-show protection?
Yes. InkStar can connect deposits and payment links to the appointment workflow, helping studios confirm serious bookings before reserving chair time.
Can I use digital consent forms and intake forms?
Yes. InkStar supports digital forms for consent, health screening fields, allergies, medical history, image consent, signatures, and appointment documentation.
Does InkStar work without internet?
InkStar is designed for offline access to essential appointment and client data. Updates sync when your device is back online.
