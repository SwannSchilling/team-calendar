FAQ
FAQs
Basic answers about how InkLinka helps tattoo studios organize clients, bookings, teams, and payments.
What is InkLinka? InkLinka is a CRM and operating system for tattoo studios. It helps teams manage clients, bookings, projects, appointments, payments, deposits, and day-to-day studio work in one place.
Who is InkLinka built for? InkLinka is built for solo tattoo artists, guest artists, studio owners, reception teams, and larger tattoo teams that need clearer control over scheduling, client records, and financial workflow.
What can I manage inside the system? You can manage client profiles, project details, appointment history, calendar events, team members, deposits, payments, invoices, personal booking links, and studio settings.
Can clients book appointments online? Yes. Studios can use booking widgets and personal links so clients can send requests or book available time, depending on the workflow the studio wants to use.
Can multiple artists work in one studio account? Yes. A studio can invite team members, assign roles, manage artist calendars, and keep appointments connected to the right client, artist, and project.
Does InkLinka support deposits and payments? Yes. InkLinka can connect deposits and payments directly to appointments and clients, helping the studio see what was paid, what is still open, and which records belong together.
Can I keep tattoo references and project context together? Yes. Projects can store the context a team needs around a tattoo request, including client details, references, session history, appointment notes, and payment information.
Do I need technical knowledge to start? No. The system is designed for studio teams to start with basic setup, add artists and clients, then expand into booking, finance, and integrations as the workflow becomes clearer.
